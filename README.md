## 🎮 About the Game

Polylithic is a third-person survival and adventure game developed by polyperfect. It launched in Early Access on November 9, 2023, and reached full release on August 25, 2026. Set in the Stone Age, it combines exploration, gathering, crafting, hunting, settlement building, tribe management, and co-op multiplayer.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Polylithic**. This trainer provides quality-of-life controls for Polylithic, including player survival boosts, resource assistance, faster progression, and settlement support. It is designed around the game's gathering, crafting, hunting, cooking, technology, companion, and tribe-management systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed Polylithic experience, faster tribe and settlement development, easier experimentation with crafting and cooking, or additional protection while exploring and hunting.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start Polylithic first, load a save, and then activate the trainer. Enable the stamina option before long journeys, hunts, or resource-gathering trips. Disable trainer functions before changing worlds, quitting, or joining a co-op session.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the resource and crafting options when preparing tools, weapons, food, buildings, or other settlement items. Gather at least one relevant material in-game before activating resource assistance so the trainer can identify the current inventory data correctly.

### World & Environment

Polylithic focuses on exploration across varied prehistoric environments, gathering resources, hunting wildlife, finding other tribes, and expanding a settlement. Survival options are most useful during extended expeditions, while technology and settlement options help accelerate progression toward the Neolithic era.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate health and stamina protection before fighting predators, large prehistoric animals, or rival tribes. Companion support can be used during hunts and dangerous encounters. Save before major battles and disable the trainer after combat if normal progression is preferred.
